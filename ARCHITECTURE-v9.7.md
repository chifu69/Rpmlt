# RP IA Enterprise v9.7 — Current Architecture

## User experience
- RP IA is the platform.
- Eagle AI is the single user-facing assistant.
- Personnel, METL, Matrix, Assessments, Corrective Actions, Knowledge Center, Reports, and Administration remain the primary user modules.

## Enterprise modules
- Server Configuration
- Diagnostic Center
- Backup & Restore
- Security and permissions

## Data model
- Personnel Master is the authoritative employee source.
- Official enterprise records are designed to live on the server.
- The PWA executes the interface, local rules, navigation, caching, and lightweight engines on the device.
